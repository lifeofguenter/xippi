Sign in: Add account, then enter the address and password above. The contact and the room are set up. Calls and replies come from a second account, signed in on another device during the review.

Blocking and reporting (guideline 1.2). Every Block and Report asks first; Report also blocks.
- One-to-one chat: right-click the chat in the sidebar > Block, or Report spam. Or View > Show inspector (⌥⌘I) > Contact > Block, or Report spam.
- Any message from someone else, in a chat, a room or a channel: right-click the message > Block sender, or Report spam.
- Room or channel member: View > Show inspector (⌥⌘I) > Members > the member's Actions button > Block, or Report spam.
- Contact requests and room invitations: in the sidebar's Requests, Block and Report spam beside Decline.
- Blocked people: Xippi > Settings (⌘,) > Accounts > the account > Blocked, each with Unblock.
