Xippi is a native Mac app for XMPP, the open chat network. Use your account on any XMPP server.

End-to-end encrypted
OMEMO encrypts one-to-one chats and private group chats.

Everything you'd ask a chat app
- Replies, reactions, edits and mentions
- Group chats and channels, with moderation
- Audio and video calls, device to device where the network allows it
- Pictures and GIFs in the chat, files and voice messages
- Several accounts side by side

A real Mac app
Notifications you reply to, a menu bar item, light and dark.
